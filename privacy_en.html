Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Soundic (the "Service") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

You provide: account info (email, display name, username, avatar), and content you post (check-ins with location, track info, photos/videos, captions, playlists, comments, likes, team and direct messages).

Collected automatically: location at check-in (shown to others rounded to roughly 100m-1km), playback history (track, artist, time, source; aggregated into rankings and recommendations), and basic device info (model, OS, app version).

2. How We Use It

To operate and improve the Service; authenticate you; display posts, follows, and notifications; power features such as Vibe Points, area rankings, and recommendations; prevent abuse; respond to inquiries; and enforce our Terms.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with your consent, when required by law, or to protect life, safety, or property.

4. Service Providers (Data Processing)

  • Supabase, Inc. (US): database, authentication, storage
  • Apple Inc. (US): music playback via MusicKit; track metadata and charts via Apple Music API / iTunes Search API
  • Google LLC (US): Gemini API for recommendations and news text (only minimal data such as mood, region, and recent track titles is sent; never your name or email)

5. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal data. Deleting your account (Settings > Delete Account) permanently removes your posts, playlists, and history.

6. Retention

Active account data is kept while your account exists. After deletion, data is removed promptly (up to 30 days for backups).

7. Security

All traffic is encrypted with TLS 1.2+; the database uses row-level security; passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth.

8. Cookies and Tracking

The app uses no cookies and no third-party ad trackers.

9. Children

The Service is intended for users aged 16 and older.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app.
